Munch Loop: Support

Questions, a problem with a purchase, or an idea for the game: write to info@sparetimevc.com. We answer in English or Russian, usually within two working days.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by the store you bought from. To restore a purchase on a new device, open the shop in the game and tap Restore purchases. Refunds: Google Play refunds through play.google.com (Order history); the App Store refunds through reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Privacy policy: https://sparetimevc.com/munch-loop/privacy

To have your data deleted, or to get a copy of it, write to info@sparetimevc.com with the subject "Data request: Munch Loop". If you bought something, add the order number (Google Play: GPA.…, App Store: the order ID from the receipt email) so we can find your purchases. Game progress is stored on your device: uninstalling the app deletes it.

Privacy

This page describes how the sparetimevc.com website handles your information.

This website

sparetimevc.com is a static website. It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind, and it does not ask you for any personal information.

The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record standard request data such as your IP address and browser type in its server logs. That data is handled under GitHub's privacy statement; we do not receive or store it.

Our products

Each product we release has its own privacy policy, which explains exactly what that product collects and why. You will find it inside the product and wherever the product is listed or downloaded.

Email

If you write to us, we keep your message so we can reply and follow up. We do not add you to any list or share your address with anyone.

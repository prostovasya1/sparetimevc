Kitty Pusher: Support

Questions, a problem with a purchase, a bug, or an idea for the game: write to info@sparetimevc.com. We answer in English or Russian, usually within two working days. If something went wrong in the game, tell us your phone model, the app version (on Android, your phone's settings > Apps > Kitty Pusher; on iPhone, the iOS version from Settings > General > About is enough) and what you did just before.

Purchases

iPhone: the App Store version sells nothing. It has no shop and no in-app purchases, so there is nothing to restore or refund, and everything in it comes from playing.

Android: the shop sells fixed items only: Remove ads, Café Patron, two décor sets and two cat packs. Each is a one-time purchase, shown in full before you buy; nothing sold is random.

  • Restore purchases: on a new phone or after reinstalling, open the shop in the game and tap Restore purchases, signed in to the same Google Play account you bought with.
  • Refunds are handled by the store you bought from: Google Play through play.google.com > Order history.
  • Parents: purchases need the store account's approval where you set it up (Google Play parental controls and Family Link).

Boxes and odds

Blind boxes come only from playing, never from purchases, on every version. The odds of every box are shown in the game: tap the odds button next to your cat shelf.

Your data

Privacy policy: https://sparetimevc.com/kitty-pusher/privacy

To change your privacy choices (where the game shows them), tap Privacy at the bottom of the home screen.

To have your data deleted, or to get a copy of it, write to info@sparetimevc.com with the subject "Data request: Kitty Pusher". If you bought something on Android, add the order number (GPA.…) so we can find your purchases. Game progress is stored on your device: uninstalling the app deletes it.
